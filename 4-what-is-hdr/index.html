What is HDR? Dolby Vision, HDR10 & HLG — iPhone vs Camera

Har naya iPhone "Dolby Vision HDR" record karne ka daava karta hai. Lekin yeh Log se kaise different hai, aur camera pe HDR kaise kaam karta hai? Poori clarity yahaan.

By Ajay K MeenaUpdated: July 20269 min read

Sabse pehle ek confusion clear karte hain jo 90% editors ko hoti hai: HDR aur Log same cheez nahi hain. Log ek acquisition tool hai (grading ke liye flat image), HDR ek delivery/display standard hai (final image ko sahi brightness ke saath screen pe dikhana). Ab detail mein samajhte hain.

HDR Kya Hai?

HDR (High Dynamic Range) ek display aur delivery standard hai jo brightness range aur color volume ko normal SDR (Standard Dynamic Range) se kaafi zyada expand karta hai. SDR displays typically 100 nits peak brightness pe capped hote hain. HDR displays 600 se 4000+ nits tak jaa sakte hain — matlab highlights zyada bright dikhte hain bina detail khoye, aur blacks zyada deep dikhte hain.

HLG sabse practical hai broadcast ke liye kyunki isko koi metadata nahi chahiye — same signal SDR display pe bhi dekh sakte ho aur HDR display pe bhi, without a separate SDR version banaye. Isiliye YouTube HDR pipeline mein HLG ka role important hai.

iPhone Mein HDR Kaise Kaam Karta Hai

Dolby Vision Profile 8.4

iPhone 12 se lekar latest models tak, jab bhi "HDR Video" setting on hoti hai (Settings → Camera → Record Video), iPhone Dolby Vision Profile 8.4 mein record karta hai — yeh 10-bit HEVC codec use karta hai aur HLG ke saath cross-compatible hai. Har frame ka apna dynamic metadata hota hai, jo capture ke time hi camera analyze karke embed karta hai — matlab tone-mapping automatically ho jaati hai per-scene basis pe.

10-bit HEVCPer-frame metadataiPhone 12+
Important distinction: Yeh Dolby Vision HDR record mode Apple Log se bilkul alag hai. Dolby Vision ek "baked-in," ready-to-view HDR result deta hai. Apple Log (iPhone 15 Pro+ only, Final Cut Camera app se) ek flat, ungraded image deta hai jo manual grading ke liye designed hai — zyada control, zyada kaam.

Camera Mein HDR Kaise Kaam Karta Hai

Mirrorless aur cinema cameras mein HDR do tareeke se milta hai:

  • HLG Picture Profile: Sony aur Panasonic bodies mein directly HLG gamma select kar sakte ho — yeh "ready to grade lightly" image deta hai jo Log se zyada contrasty hoti hai but SDR-compatible bhi rehti hai.
  • HDR-PQ Mode (Canon): Kuch Canon bodies mein HDR PQ (Perceptual Quantizer) mode hota hai jo directly Rec.2100-PQ standard mein record karta hai — mostly straight-to-delivery HDR content ke liye, grading ke liye nahi.

Benefits of HDR

  • Highlights bina clip hue bright dikhte hain (sun, lights, reflections)
  • Deeper, richer blacks — better perceived contrast
  • Wider color volume — saturated colors bright areas mein bhi maintain hote hain
  • Real-world jaisa viewing experience compatible displays pe

Practical Trap: YouTube HDR Upload

Jab tum HDR video YouTube pe upload karte ho, platform automatically ek SDR trim-pass generate karta hai taaki normal screens pe bhi video theek dikhe. Agar tum control chahte ho ki SDR version kaisa dikhega, toh khud LUT ya trim pass supply karo export ke time — warna YouTube ka auto-conversion kabhi flat, kabhi over-saturated result de sakta hai.

FAQs

HDR aur Log profile same hain kya?

Nahi. Log ek acquisition curve hai jo grading ke liye flat image deta hai. HDR ek delivery/display standard hai jo final graded image ko wide luminance range ke saath screen pe accurately dikhata hai. Dono alag purpose serve karte hain.

iPhone HDR video record karta hai matlab kya?

iPhone 12 aur uske baad ke models Dolby Vision Profile 8.4 mein record karte hain — yeh HLG ke saath backward-compatible hai aur per-frame dynamic metadata carry karta hai jo compatible displays pe automatically apply hoti hai.

YouTube pe HDR video upload karne se kya hota hai SDR viewers ke liye?

YouTube automatically ek SDR trim-pass generate karta hai HDR upload se, taaki normal screens pe bhi video sahi dikhe. Aap khud ka SDR trim ya LUT bhi supply kar sakte ho control ke liye.
