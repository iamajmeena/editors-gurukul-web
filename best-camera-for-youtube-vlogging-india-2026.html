Vlogging Camera Choose Karte Waqt Kya Dekhein

1. Flip/Vari-Angle Screen

Non-negotiable feature — bina flip screen ke khud ko frame karna guesswork ban jaata hai.

2. Autofocus Reliability

Solo shooting mein agar autofocus miss karta hai toh reshoot ke sivaay option nahi bachta. Face/eye-tracking must-have hai.

3. Audio Quality

Built-in mic decent honi chahiye backup ke liye, lekin external mic input hamesha priority honi chahiye serious content ke liye.

4. Battery Life

Lambi recording sessions ke liye spare batteries ya USB-C power delivery support dekhna zaroori hai.

Quick FAQ

Q: YouTube vlogging ke liye best budget camera kaunsi hai India mein?

Sony ZV-1F sabse affordable option hai jo true Log support deta hai. Better all-round quality ke liye Sony ZV-E10 II best value hai higher budget mein.

Q: Kya smartphone se vlogging kaafi nahi hai?

Beginners ke liye smartphone theek hai, lekin dedicated camera background blur, better low-light, external mic support aur manual control deti hai jo growth ke liye zaroori hote hain.

Q: Fixed-lens camera behtar hai ya interchangeable lens?

Fixed-lens (G7X, ZV-1F) simplicity aur portability deta hai. Interchangeable lens (ZV-E10 II) long-term flexibility aur better image quality potential deta hai.

Q: Kya in cameras se professional-looking YouTube content banaya ja sakta hai?

Haan, agar aap sahi lighting, audio aur basic color grading ka combination use karte ho, in sab cameras se professional-level content possible hai.
