4 Exposure & Color Metrics Every Cameraman Must Know

1. Nits Brightness vs Direct Sunlight: 500 Nits monitors indoor/shade ke liye perfect hain, lekin outdoor afternoon sunlight me sunhood ke bina washed out ho jate hain. 2800 Nits (Desview R6) direct sunlight me saaf dikhta hai.
2. False Color IRE Chart for S-Log3 / C-Log2: Skin tones ko perfect expose karne ke liye False Color tool 40-45 IRE (Green) par Wheatish skin tone aur 60-65 IRE (Pink) par Fair skin tone dikhata hai. Blown-out highlights Red (100+ IRE) dikhte hain.
3. Waveform (RGB Parade): Waveform graph me exact positioning pata chalti hai ki kaunsa frame corner overexposed hai.
4. 3D LUT Support (.CUBE): SD card se custom 33x33 `.CUBE` LUT load karke screen par graded look live preview kar sakte hain.

FAQs

False Color me skin tone par konsa color aana chahiye?

S-Log3 ya C-Log2 me shoot karte waqt skin par Green / Pink (40 - 65 IRE) dikhna chahiye. Agar skin Red dikh rahi hai, toh face overexposed (clipped) ho chuka hai.
