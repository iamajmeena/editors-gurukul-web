Best Mirrorless Camera Under ₹1,00,000 in India — Top 3 Picks for Filmmakers (2026)

₹1 lakh ke budget mein sabse balanced mirrorless pick hai Sony ZV-E10 II — agar aapko flip-out screen, achhi autofocus aur real 4K10-bit chahiye. Lekin agar stills bhi serious hain toh Canon EOS R50 aur Fujifilm X-S20 bhi strong contenders hain.

Quick take: Solo content creators ke liye Sony ZV-E10 II sabse safe bet hai. Agar hybrid stills+video chahiye toh Fujifilm X-S20 (film simulations + IBIS) best hai. Aur agar Canon ecosystem mein already invest ho chuke ho toh EOS R50 fits perfectly.

1. Sony ZV-E10 II — The All-Rounder Vlogging Machine

#1 · BEST OVERALL
Sony ZV-E10 II (Body/Kit)
~₹95,000
26MP APS-C4K/60p no cropS-Log2/S-Log310-bit 4:2:2Product Showcase AFMic + headphone jack

Yeh camera ZV-E10 ka seedha upgrade hai — sensor 26MP hua, processor BIONZ XR aaya, aur sabse bada difference: ab 4K/60p bina crop ke milta hai. Yeh feature khud iss price bracket mein rare hai. E-mount hone ki wajah se Sony ke poore APS-C aur full-frame lens ecosystem tak access milta hai.

Colorist ka nazariya: 10-bit 4:2:2 internal recording ka matlab hai grading mein banding ka risk kaafi kam ho jaata hai — heavy secondary grades bhi yeh footage handle kar leta hai. S-Log3 shoot karke white balance ko DaVinci Resolve mein sahi lock karna seekh lo toh output professional level tak jaa sakta hai.

Pros

  • Bina crop 4K/60p — is price range mein unusual
  • 10-bit 4:2:2 internal — grading-friendly
  • Headphone jack + mic input dono
  • Reliable subject-tracking autofocus

Cons

  • No viewfinder (rear screen only)
  • Body plasticky, not weather-sealed
  • Battery life average — spare battery zaroori

Colorist ka nazariya: F-Log2 wide dynamic range capture karta hai, aur agar aap Log footage grading ke fundamentals samajhte ho toh yeh sensor bahut room deta hai skin tones ke liye. Open-gate 6.2K recording ka matlab hai reframing flexibility bhi milti hai post mein.

Kya Dekhna Chahiye ₹1 Lakh Ke Budget Mein

1. 10-bit vs 8-bit Recording

Is budget range mein 10-bit internal recording milna shuru ho jaata hai (jaise ZV-E10 II mein) — yeh heavy grading ke liye game-changer hai kyunki banding aur color-shift risk kaafi kam ho jaata hai.

2. Autofocus Reliability

Solo shooting ya interviews ke liye eye-tracking autofocus non-negotiable hai. Canon aur Sony dono strong hain yahaan, Fujifilm thoda peeche.

3. Ecosystem aur Lens Availability

Jis brand ka lens ecosystem local market mein zyada easily milta hai (rent ya buy), usi brand mein invest karna long-term mein sasta padta hai.

4. IBIS (In-Body Image Stabilisation)

Agar bina gimbal ke handheld shoot karna hai, toh IBIS wali body (Fujifilm X-S20) kaafi farak dalti hai.

Quick FAQ

Q: ₹1 lakh mein sabse achhi mirrorless camera kaunsi hai filmmaking ke liye?

Sony ZV-E10 II sabse balanced pick hai — bina crop 4K/60p, 10-bit recording aur S-Log profiles milte hain is budget mein.

Q: Kya Fujifilm X-S20 beginners ke liye sahi hai?

Haan, lekin menu system thoda different hai Sony/Canon se. Colour science aur IBIS ke liye yeh best hai agar learning curve se dikkat nahi.

Q: Canon EOS R50 mein Log profile kyun nahi hai?

Canon apne Log profiles higher-end R-series bodies (R7, R6 II, R5) ke liye reserve karta hai. R50 entry-level positioning follow karta hai.

Q: Kya is budget mein full-frame camera milegi?

Nahi, ₹1 lakh mein full-frame sensor wali reliable video-first camera milna mushkil hai — APS-C hi best value deta hai is range mein.
