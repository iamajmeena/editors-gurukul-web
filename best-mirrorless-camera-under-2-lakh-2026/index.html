₹2 lakh ke budget mein Sony a6700 sabse complete package hai — 10-bit 4:2:2, S-Log3, aur best-in-class autofocus. Lekin agar full-frame low-light performance chahiye toh Panasonic Lumix S9 bhi consider karna banta hai.

Quick take: All-round video work ke liye Sony a6700 lo. Stills + video hybrid aur resolution priority hai toh Fujifilm X-T5. Full-frame look aur low-light chahiye budget mein toh Panasonic Lumix S9.

₹2 Lakh Range Mein Kya Priority Karein

1. Full-Frame vs APS-C Trade-off

Is budget mein full-frame milna shuru ho jaata hai (Lumix S9), lekin APS-C bodies (a6700, X-T5) autofocus aur burst performance mein aage rehte hain. Depends on your priority — sensor size ka detailed impact yahaan samjho.

2. Codec aur Bitrate

Teeno cameras high-bitrate 10-bit codecs support karte hain — matlab footage grading ke liye kaafi headroom milta hai, especially skies aur skin tones mein.

3. Ecosystem Lock-in

Sony aur Fujifilm ka lens ecosystem India mein sabse zyada mature hai — rental aur second-hand availability dono better hai compared to Panasonic L-mount.

Quick FAQ

Q: ₹2 lakh mein best video-first mirrorless camera kaunsi hai?

Sony a6700 sabse complete package hai — AI autofocus, 10-bit 4:2:2, IBIS aur S-Log profiles is budget mein best combination denge.

Q: Kya is budget mein full-frame camera lena sahi rahega?

Haan, Panasonic Lumix S9 ek achha full-frame option hai lekin autofocus aur viewfinder ki kami jaise trade-offs samajh kar decide karo.

Q: Fujifilm X-T5 video ke liye sahi hai ya sirf stills ke liye?

Dono ke liye achha hai — 6.2K open-gate video capability strong hai, lekin video-specific autofocus Sony se thoda peeche hai.

Q: Kya ₹2 lakh mein cinema camera better option hoga mirrorless se?

Nahi, is budget mein mirrorless zyada versatile hai kyunki stills, autofocus aur portability bhi milti hai jo dedicated cinema cameras nahi deti.
