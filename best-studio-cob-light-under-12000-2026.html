3 Things Brands Hide in Spec Sheets

1. CRI vs TLCI (Skin Tone Color Accuracy): CRI 90+ har brand likhta hai, lekin TLCI metric sensor accuracy dikhata hai. Godox ka TLCI 97+ hai, jisse skin tones post-production me natural rehti hain.
2. Bi-Color Power Penalty: Bi-color lights me jab aap 3200K ya 6500K set karte hain, aadhe LEDs OFF rehte hain — 250W light real me ~130W output deti hai.
3. Fan Noise vs Mic Distance: High-wattage (250W-300W) lights me cooling fans loud ho sakte hain. Sensitve condenser mic ko light se 5-6 feet door rakhein.

FAQs

DaVinci Resolve me cheap COB light ki green tint kaise fix karein?
