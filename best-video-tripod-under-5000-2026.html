The "Fake Fluid Head" Myth: Read This Before Buying

Agar aap Amazon India par ₹2,000 se ₹3,000 ke beech koi tripod dekhenge, toh har doosra brand apne 3-way pan head ko "Fluid Head" bol kar bechta hai. Lekin sach ye hai ki unme fluid nahi — sasta sticky grease hota hai. Jab aap camera pan karoge, toh shuru me zhatka (jerk) lagta hai aur video me jitter aata hai.

Real fluid head me constant resistance drag hota hai. Is guide me humne sirf wahi models shortlist kiye hain jo Indian budget video creators ke liye genuinely smooth video movement dete hain.

Buying Checklist: Sasta Video Tripod Lete Waqt Kya Dekhein?

  1. Weight Load Capacity: Apne camera + lens ka total weight naapein. Tripod ki capacity aapke setup se double honi chahiye.
  2. Mid-Level Spreader: Tripod legs ke beech me spreader hona chahiye taaki legs outdoor hawa me phel na jaayein.
  3. Cold Weather Note: Sardi ke mausam me cheap fluid heads stiff ho sakte hain — shoot se pehle head ko 2-3 baar full rotate karke warmup kar lein.

FAQs

Kya in tripods se low-angle ground shots le sakte hain?

In mid-level spreader tripods ka center column ulta nahi hota. Low-angle ground shots ke liye fluid head ko alag karke ek chote mini-tripod (jaise Joby ya Digitek Gorilla pod) par mount karna sabse best tarika hai.
