Why Cheap Wireless Mics Sound "Robotic"?

Agar aap Amazon par ₹2,000 ke paas wale wireless mic buy karte hain, toh sabse zyaada log ek hi cheez complain karte hain: "Jaise hi noise cancellation button dabate hain, aawaz paani ke andar se ya robot jaisi aane lagti hai."

Noise cancellation ke chakkar me cheap DSP chips background fan noise ke saath aapki vocal warmth bhi kaat dete hain. Studio ya indoor room me hamesha Noise Cancellation OFF rakhein aur room ka fan slow kar dein. ANC ko tabhi ON karein jab outdoor heavy road noise ho.
