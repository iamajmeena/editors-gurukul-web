25 New DaVinci Resolve 21 Features You Need to Know (2026)

Meta Description: DaVinci Resolve 21's biggest update yet, broken down — AI tools, the new Photo page, Color page upgrades, Fusion, Fairlight, and content-creator features every editor should know in 2026.

Focus Keywords: DaVinci Resolve 21 new features, DaVinci Resolve 21 update, DaVinci Resolve AI tools 2026, DaVinci Resolve Photo page

Written by Ajay K Meena — Cinematographer, Colorist & Director, Wedream Production

The Biggest Resolve Update in Years

Blackmagic Design released DaVinci Resolve 21 in June 2026, and it isn't a routine version bump — it's over 100 new motion graphics, 50+ major features, and hundreds of smaller quality-of-life fixes spread across every page of the app. A brand-new Photo page, a wave of genuinely useful AI tools, and Color page upgrades that change how professional colorists structure a grade.

Most "what's new" roundups just list feature names. This one explains what each feature actually does, which page it lives on, and whether it's something you'll use every week or only occasionally — so you're not wasting time exploring menus that don't matter for your workflow.

AI Tools — The Neural Engine Gets a Major Upgrade

1AI IntelliSearch

Search your entire project using plain language instead of manual tagging. Type a description, a keyword from spoken dialogue, or even search for a specific person's face, and IntelliSearch returns matching clips directly in the Media Pool. For anyone editing long-form footage — weddings, interviews, event coverage — this alone can save hours of scrubbing.

2AI Speech Generator

Generate a synthetic voiceover from text using a built-in Blackmagic voice model, or clone a unique voice from as little as a 10-second sample. You can adjust speed, pitch, and inflection to create multiple takes without re-recording. Useful for scratch narration, temp voiceovers on client review cuts, or accessibility dubs.

3AI CineFocus

Simulate depth of field entirely in post — click to set a focal point, then adjust aperture and focal range to change how much of the frame is in focus. Advanced controls even let you pick the bokeh shape and keyframe the focal point for a rack-focus effect, all without a single roto mask.

4AI Face Age Transformer

Add or remove age-related features — wrinkles, facial fullness — on a tracked face, with an adjustable age-offset slider. Built specifically for continuity in flashback and flash-forward scenes, which used to require actual VFX compositing work.

5AI Blemish Removal

Reduces acne, discoloration, spots, and pores on a tracked face while preserving natural skin texture — not the plastic, over-smoothed look older beauty tools produced. A strength slider controls intensity per subject.

6AI Slate ID

Automatically detects and extracts clapperboard metadata from your footage — even in dark or slightly out-of-focus slate shots — and logs it without manual entry. On any shoot with more than a handful of takes, this genuinely saves hours during ingest.

7AI UltraSharpen

Resolve's most advanced sharpening tool yet, built specifically to rescue upscaled or slightly soft footage. Works well paired with Super Scale for old client footage or social re-purposing where the source resolution is lower than your delivery target.

8AI Motion Deblur

Analyzes a clip and generates a new render with motion-blur streaks and softness significantly reduced — particularly useful for cleaning up footage you plan to slow down or freeze-frame, where existing motion blur becomes obvious and distracting.

Photo Page — A Genuinely New Part of Resolve

9Full Node-Based Grading for Stills

The headline feature of Resolve 21: an entirely new Photo page bringing the same node-based color engine used on major motion pictures to still images. Primary correction, curves, qualifiers, Power Windows, and the full node editor are all available on photos now — not a stripped-down version.

10Albums and LightBox View

Organize photos into albums by shoot day, camera, or any criteria you want, then use LightBox view to see your entire album with grades applied simultaneously — grade one image live and watch the look update across the whole collection in real time if it's linked. Filter by graded/ungraded, star rating, or flag for fast culling.

11Direct Camera Tethering

Tether a supported Sony or Canon camera directly to Resolve for live capture — adjust ISO, exposure, and white balance, monitor live, and save capture presets so your look is locked in before you shoot. Captured images land straight into an album.

Edit & Cut Pages — Faster Everyday Workflow

12New Ease Keyframe Modes

Loop, pingpong, and relative easing modes mean you can build repeating animations without manually duplicating keyframes. Combined with four-point Bezier easing in the curves editor, this brings genuinely cinema-quality retiming control to a part of Resolve that used to feel clunky compared to After Effects.

13Animate Fusion Effects Without Leaving Edit

You can now adjust Fusion effects — text, transitions, motion graphics — directly in the Keyframes and Curves editors on the Cut and Edit pages. No more jumping to the Fusion page for small tweaks; click through only when you need the deeper toolset.

14Smart Bin Views in the Cut Page

The Cut page's bin dropdown now supports Smart Bins — sophisticated Media Pool filters based on rules like file type, flag color, or metadata. Example: a bin that auto-populates with only green-flagged .braw clips, updating live as you tag footage.

Color Page — Structural Upgrades for Real Grading Work

15MultiMaster Trim Manager

Generate multiple HDR and SDR trim deliverables from a single timeline. The node editor displays additional trim layers so you can color-manage each output standard separately and render every version in one pass — a big deal if you deliver to both broadcast and streaming specs.

16Magic Mask Render in Place

Cache a tracked Magic Mask as a traveling matte node that links automatically to your active node. This drastically lightens playback processing while you keep grading in real time, instead of the timeline chugging every time Magic Mask has to re-track.

17Layer List View in the Node Editor

An alternative to the traditional node graph — nodes display as rows in a list, numbered by their position in the chain. Less visual clutter, and genuinely easier for labelling, locking, bypassing, or reordering nodes on a complex grade with 15+ nodes.

18Group Color Grade Versions

Groups can now hold multiple grade versions the same way individual clips can. Right-click to add a version or switch the group's pre-clip/post-clip node graph, applying the change uniformly across every clip in that group — useful for A/B testing a look across an entire scene at once.

Fusion — Motion Graphics Get a Library

19Krokodove Motion Graphics Library

Fusion now ships with Krokodove, a large and growing library of motion graphics — utility tools, vector/data tools, and customizable 2D/3D templates usable throughout Resolve, not just inside Fusion. This is the fastest route to polished intro/outro graphics without building from scratch.

20Audio-Driven Fusion Animation

The new Fairlight Animator modifier connects Fusion directly to Fairlight's audio engine — audio levels from a clip can now drive animated parameters automatically. Think lips or eyes animating in sync with dialogue or music without manual keyframing.

Fairlight — Audio Workflow Cleanup

21Manage Audio Tracks with Folders

Group audio tracks into a collapsible folder to save timeline space — collapsed, it shows mini rectangles representing the contained clips' count and duration. Expand any time you need individual track access. A genuine relief on projects with 15+ audio tracks.

22EQ and Level Matcher

Two new tools that adjust a target clip's audio to match a reference clip automatically — EQ Match tracks tonal spectrum dynamically across the clip, and Level Matcher intercuts sections seamlessly without manual gain riding. Built for multi-mic or multi-location dialogue matching.

Content Creator Tools — Built for YouTube and Instagram

23Vertical and Square Resolution Presets

Project and timeline settings now include dedicated square and vertical resolution options for TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, X, and Snapchat, and they pair directly with Smart Reframe for fast vertical conversion of horizontally-shot footage.

24Upload Directly to Social Media

Link your YouTube, TikTok, Vimeo, and X accounts and export straight to the platform from within Resolve. Compression settings are pre-tuned per platform's own recommendations, so you're not guessing at bitrate settings for each destination.

25Improved Object Tracking for Secondary Grades

The tracker palette now follows objects and people in 3D space more reliably when a Power Window is placed on a subject, producing a cleaner mask for secondary grading or effects — the same tracking backbone used in the skin-isolation technique in our white balance guide.

Quick FAQ

Q: Is DaVinci Resolve 21 free or Studio-only?
Most of Resolve 21's core updates (Photo page, Layer List view, keyframe improvements) are available in the free version. The heavier AI tools — Face Age Transformer, Blemish Removal, UltraSharpen, Motion Deblur — are Studio-exclusive. See our Studio vs Free breakdown for the full comparison.

Q: When was DaVinci Resolve 21 released?
Blackmagic Design released the final version in June 2026, after being announced at NAB earlier that year.

Q: What's the single most useful new feature for content creators?
Vertical/square resolution presets paired with Smart Reframe, plus direct social media upload — together they cut a real chunk of time out of the export-and-distribute step of every video.

Q: Do I need a powerful PC to run the new AI tools?
Yes — the Neural Engine features are GPU-accelerated and noticeably faster on newer hardware. Check our system requirements guide if you're planning an upgrade.
