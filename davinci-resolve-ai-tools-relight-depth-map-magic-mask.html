DaVinci Resolve AI Tools Masterclass: Relight, Depth Map & Magic Mask 2.0

AI features in DaVinci Resolve are not gimmicks — they save hours of tedious roto and green screen masking. Here is your practical production guide to mastering Relight 3D, Depth Map, Magic Mask 2.0, and Surface Tracker in DaVinci Resolve 21.

👤 By Ajay K. Meena (Cinematographer & Colorist)⏱️ 9 min read📅 Updated 2026

⚡ Summary of AI Power Tools in DaVinci 21

  • AI Relight FX: Generates a virtual 3D normal map of your shot, letting you place virtual point lights and directional key lights on actors post-shoot.
  • Depth Map FX: Automatically extracts a 32-bit depth matte separating foreground actors from background environments in one click.
  • Magic Mask 2.0: Tracks people, clothing, features, or objects across fast movement without manual rotoscoping.
  • Pro Performance Rule: Always Render Cache AI nodes (Right-click node > Node Render Cache > On) to prevent GPU VRAM lag during playback.

1. AI Relight 3D: Adding Post-Production Studio Lighting

Ever wished you could add a subtle rim light or key light on an actor's face after you wrap the shoot? **DaVinci Relight FX** calculates surface normal maps of objects and human faces in 2D video footage.

💡 How to Setup AI Relight FX

  1. Add an empty serial node on your Color Page. Drag Relight from the OpenFX library onto the node.
  2. Set Surface Normals Mode to Generate Normals (or link an external Surface Normal map if generated in Blender/Unreal Engine).
  3. Choose light type: Directional Light, Point Light, or Spot Light.
  4. Position the 3D target handle in your viewer window. Adjust Light Contrast, Smoothness, and Specular Brightness.
  5. Use color wheels on the same node to tint the virtual light (e.g. warm golden hour sunset glow or cool teal backlight).

2. Depth Map FX: Instant Foreground / Background Separation

Traditionally, creating atmospheric haze, background blur (fake bokeh), or darkening backgrounds behind actors required hours of manual rotoscoping. **Depth Map FX** uses neural depth estimation to calculate distance per pixel.

🌫️ Adding Background Haze & Fog

Invert the Depth Map mask so only far background pixels are targeted. Add a slight Lift/Offset boost and lower saturation to create realistic atmospheric depth cue in exterior shots.

🎯 Isolating Busy Backgrounds

Desaturate and lower exposure on the background node while keeping the foreground actor crisp and bright. This immediately pulls viewer focus directly to your subject.

3. Magic Mask 2.0: AI Rotoscoping for Subjects & Objects

DaVinci Resolve 21's updated **Magic Mask 2.0** uses multi-frame optical flow and AI tracking to lock onto subjects even when they cross behind obstacles or turn around.

🎯 Magic Mask Best Practices

  • Person Mode vs Object Mode: Select Person Mode when tracking human subjects (allows targeting Person, Face, Hair, Arms, Torso, or Clothing separately). Select Object Mode for cars, products, or props.
  • Stroke Guidance: Draw a single blue positive stroke across the center of your subject. Only draw red negative strokes if the mask accidentally leaks onto nearby objects.
  • Track Forward & Backward: Click Track Forward and Backward. DaVinci Neural Engine will build tracking points at up to 60fps.
  • Matte Finesse: Always add 1.0 to 2.0 pixels of Blur Radius and set Clean Black to 5.0 to eliminate edge chatter around moving hair or clothing.

4. Surface Tracker: Locking Logos & Graphics to Moving Clothes

Unlike rigid point trackers, **Surface Tracker FX** creates a dynamic warping 3D mesh grid over flexible surfaces like t-shirts, flags, skin, or blowing curtains.

You can replace logos on t-shirts, add tattoos to skin, or swap out artwork on moving banners without any 3D tracking software.

💡 GPU VRAM & Node Cache Performance Hack

AI FX nodes (especially Relight and Depth Map) demand heavy GPU VRAM. To avoid timeline lag or GPU Memory Full errors: Right-click the AI node > select Node Render Cache > On. Once cached, the node turn blue in your node tree and plays back at a locked 24fps without using GPU power during playback!

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are these AI features available in DaVinci Resolve Free?

Basic Magic Mask features are available in Free, but Relight 3D, Depth Map FX, and Surface Tracker are exclusive to DaVinci Resolve Studio (which unlocks DaVinci Neural Engine 3D hardware acceleration).

Q: What GPU specifications are required for smooth AI tracking in Resolve 21?

We recommend an Apple Silicon Mac (M1/M2/M3/M4 Pro/Max with Unified Memory) or an NVIDIA RTX 4060/4070 GPU with at least 8GB to 12GB VRAM for smooth 4K AI processing.
