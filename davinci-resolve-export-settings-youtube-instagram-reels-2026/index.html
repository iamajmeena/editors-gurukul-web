How to Export in DaVinci Resolve for YouTube, Instagram & Reels

Grade perfect thi, timeline mein colors bilkul sahi lag rahe the — phir upload karte hi washed out ya blurry ho gaya? Fix aapki grade mein nahi, Delivery page settings mein hai.

By Ajay K MeenaUpdated: July 202615 min read

Yeh sabse common complaint hai jo mujhe milti hai — "Sir grade toh perfect thi Resolve mein, YouTube pe upload karte hi washed out ho gayi." Ya phir — "Reels pe blurry kyun aa raha hai, maine toh 4K mein export kiya tha." Dono problems ka source same hai: Delivery page settings platform ke expectations se match nahi kar rahe. Chaliye exact settings dekhte hain — YouTube, Instagram Reels, aur TikTok, teeno ke liye — aur samajhte hain yeh problems actually hoti kyun hain.

Export Upload Ke Baad Alag Kyun Dikhta Hai

Char cheezein decide karti hain ki upload ke baad video kaisi dikhegi: codec (video kaise compress hua), bitrate (per-second kitna data allot hua), color space/gamma tag (brightness aur color kaise interpret hua), aur platform ka apna recompression (har platform upload ke baad khud se re-encode karta hai).

Sabse common culprit — gamma shift — hai. Resolve do Rec.709 flavors deta hai: Rec.709 Gamma 2.4 (broadcast standard) aur Rec.709-A (Apple-friendly variant, alag gamma curve). YouTube har upload ko Rec.709-A maan leta hai, chahe aapne kuch bhi tag kiya ho — isiliye export QuickTime (Mac) mein perfect lagta hai but Chrome (Windows) mein washed out.

Doosra culprit mismatched bitrate hai — 4K, 80,000 Kb/s ka YouTube export Instagram Reel ke liye overkill hai aur heavier compression trigger karta hai jo video ko sharp nahi, soft banata hai. Teesra — wrong codec choose karna. Chautha — wrong resolution export karna, especially 4K ko Instagram/TikTok bhejna jo short-form vertical video ke liye natively support nahi karte.

Export se pehle yeh do settings check karo: Preferences → General mein "Automatically tag Rec.709 Scene clips as Rec.709-A" toggle karo, aur Project Settings → Color Management mein Timeline color space set karo — Rec.709-A (Apple-heavy audience) ya Rec.709 Gamma 2.4 (cross-platform, Windows/Android/Smart TV audience). Yeh do settings 80% gamma-shift problems solve kar dete hain export se pehle hi.

YouTube Export Settings (16:9, Long-Form)

Deliver page open karo (Shift+8), Custom export choose karo:

Format & Codec

Format: MP4 ya QuickTime (dono chalte hain — MP4 wider compatibility deta hai, QuickTime metadata better preserve karta hai). Codec: H.264 — YouTube apni taraf se sab kuch VP9/AV1 mein re-encode karta hai, but H.264 accept sabse reliably karta hai. Encoder: Auto ya apni GPU (NVENC Windows/Nvidia pe, VideoToolbox Apple Silicon pe).

MP4 / QuickTimeH.264Hardware encoder recommended

Resolution, Frame Rate & Bitrate

Resolution: 3840×2160 (4K), chahe source 1080p ho — YouTube 4K-tagged uploads ko zyada bitrate allocate karta hai, isliye upscaled 4K export native 1080p se bhi sharp aa sakta hai. Frame rate: timeline se match karo. Bitrate: 4K ke liye 80,000 Kb/s, 1080p ke liye 30,000 Kb/s "Restrict to" pe set karo. 100,000 Kb/s se upar jaane ka koi fayda nahi — sirf upload time badhega.

3840×216080,000 Kb/s (4K)Multi-pass encode: ON

Advanced — Color & Audio

Color space tag: Rec.709. Gamma tag: Rec.709-A (Mac timelines) ya Gamma 2.4 (cross-platform). Data levels: Video. Force sizing/debayer to highest quality: ON. Audio: AAC, 320 Kb/s, 48 kHz — YouTube ka broadcast standard.

Rec.709AAC 320 Kb/s / 48kHz

Free version 4K tak 60fps 8-bit export karta hai — 99% YouTube creators ke liye kaafi hai. Studio 10-bit, 4K se upar resolutions, 120fps aur Neural Engine unlock karta hai.

Instagram Reels Settings (9:16 Vertical)

Reels alag beast hai — Instagram aggressively recompress karta hai, aur 4K natively support nahi karta, isliye "zyada bitrate = better quality" formula yahan reverse ho jaata hai.

Format, Codec & Resolution

Format: MP4. Codec: H.264. Resolution: 1080×1920 — Deliver page mein "Use vertical resolution" toggle on karo. Frame rate: 30fps (60fps avoid karo, Instagram usse waapas 30 pe strip kar deta hai).

1080×192030fpsNetwork optimization: OFF

Bitrate & The 50MB Rule

Restrict to: 12,000–15,000 Kb/s. Rate control: CBR (Constant Bitrate — Reels ke liye upload reliability better hoti hai VBR se). Sabse important rule: final file 50MB se neeche rakho. Isse upar files ko Instagram heavier compression se hit karta hai — yehi asli reason hai blurry Reels ka, bitrate kam hone se zyada. Agar file 50MB cross kare, bitrate 1,000 Kb/s kam karo aur re-export karo.

12,000–15,000 Kb/sUnder 50MB targetCBR
Advanced tag: Color space Rec.709, Gamma tag 2.2 (mobile-first viewing) ya Rec.709-A (agar audience mostly iPhone hai). Audio: AAC, 320 Kb/s, 48kHz stereo.

TikTok Export Settings

TikTok ka compression behavior Reels jaisa hi hai, but file-size pe thoda zyada forgiving — hard upload cap 287MB hai, lekin 70MB se upar heavier compression start ho jaata hai. Isliye target wahi rakho: 1080×1920, H.264, 12,000–15,000 Kb/s, 30fps (ya cinematic feel ke liye 24fps), file 50MB se neeche.

Gamma Shift — Washed Out Colors Ka Asli Fix

Yahi Reddit aur Blackmagic forums pe sabse zyada pooche jaane wala Resolve question hai. Jab aap Resolve mein grade karte ho, aapka display profile use hota hai colors interpret karne ke liye. Export karte waqt Rec.709-A gamma tag lagate ho toh QuickTime (Mac) exactly wahi image dikhata hai. Lekin YouTube upload ke baad har file ko Rec.709-A assume kar leta hai — Mac pe colors match ho jaate hain, lekin Windows/Android pe nahi, kyunki wahan gamma curve differently interpret hota hai.

Do valid workflows hain — apni audience ke hisaab se choose karo:

Apple-First Workflow

  • Audience mostly iPhone/Mac ho toh yeh use karo
  • Timeline color space: Rec.709-A
  • Output color space: Rec.709-A
  • Export gamma tag: Rec.709-A

Cross-Platform Workflow

  • Broadly mixed audience (Windows/Android/Smart TV) ke liye safer
  • Timeline color space: Rec.709 Gamma 2.4
  • Output color space: Rec.709 Gamma 2.4
  • Export gamma tag: Gamma 2.4
Quick test: Ek sample clip export karo, QuickTime mein dekho, phir unlisted YouTube video ke roop mein upload karke Chrome (Windows/Android) pe dekho. Agar YouTube version darker ya crushed lag raha hai QuickTime version se, toh gamma tag aapki audience ke display se fight kar raha hai.

Bada file size better social upload guarantee nahi karta. Ek certain bitrate ke baad, extra bits platform ke recompressor se discard ho jaate hain — sirf upload time badhta hai, quality nahi.

Trial-and-export workflow: Recommended range ke high end pe export karo → file size check karo → agar target se upar hai, bitrate 1,000 Kb/s kam karke re-export karo → target ke just neeche aane tak repeat karo.

Audio Loudness Targets

Sabhi platforms ke liye universal audio setting: AAC, stereo, 320 Kb/s CBR, 48kHz. Loudness targets: YouTube -14 LUFS integrated, Instagram/TikTok roughly -16 LUFS. Fairlight page ke Loudness Meter (View → Show Loudness Meter) se apna timeline check karo aur master fader adjust karo jab tak target ke 1 LU ke andar na aa jaaye.

Hardware Encoder Choose Karna — Mac vs PC

Deliver page ka Encoder dropdown decide karta hai ki compression ka kaam CPU karega ya GPU. Hardware encoding dramatically fast hai (often 3-5x), lekin extreme high-bitrate scenarios mein software encoding se thoda kam efficient ho sakta hai.

  • Apple Silicon Macs (M1-M4): VideoToolbox choose karo — Apple ke dedicated media engine ka use karta hai, sabse fast option bina visible quality loss ke.
  • Windows + Nvidia GPU (RTX 20-series+): NVENC choose karo, H.264 aur H.265 dono ke liye. "Native" software encoding pe switch mat karo jab tak extremely low bitrates pe export nahi kar rahe jahan har bit count karta hai.
  • Windows + AMD GPU: VCE/AMF choose karo. Purane AMD cards ka H.264 hardware encoder weak ho sakta hai — agar export soft lag raha hai, ek test "Native" software encoding se try karo.
  • Linux: "Native" software encoding hi reliable option hai.

Free version users disadvantage mein nahi hain — yeh sabhi encoders Free version mein bhi available hain, Studio-exclusive nahi.

Custom Preset Save Karna — Ek Baar Setup, Hamesha Reuse

Deliver page mein settings dial-in karne ke baad, render settings panel ke top-right corner mein teen dots pe click karo → "Save as new preset" → descriptively naam do: "YouTube 4K H.264," "Reels 1080p Vertical," "TikTok 1080p Vertical." Agli baar se sirf preset select karo, in/out points set karo, aur render — teen presets, teen platforms, zero guessing.

FAQs

YouTube pe export washed out kyun dikh raha hai?

Almost hamesha gamma shift ki wajah se. YouTube har upload ko Rec.709-A assume kar leta hai chahe aapne kuch bhi tag kiya ho. Fix: timeline aur export tag dono ko Rec.709-A (Apple-heavy audience) ya dono ko Rec.709 Gamma 2.4 (cross-platform audience) set karo — mix mat karo.

Instagram Reels ke liye kaunsa bitrate use karein?

12,000–15,000 Kb/s CBR ke saath. Sabse important rule: final file 50MB se neeche rakho — usse upar files ko Instagram heavier compression se hit karta hai, jo actual reason hai blurry uploads ka.

DaVinci Resolve Free version YouTube ke liye kaafi hai?

Haan. Free version 4K UHD tak 60fps mein H.264 aur H.265 dono codecs ke saath export karta hai — 99% YouTube creators ke liye sufficient. Studio sirf 10-bit precision, 4K se upar resolutions, 120fps ya ProRes delivery chahiye ho tab zaroori hai.

Social media exports ke liye CBR ya VBR?

CBR use karo — teeno platforms (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok) ke liye. CBR steady data rate rakhta hai jo upload reliability improve karta hai aur heavy recompression pe occasional audio sync drift avoid karta hai. VBR archival/offline delivery ke liye fine hai.

Vertical video kaise export karein DaVinci Resolve mein?

Project Settings mein timeline resolution 1080×1920 set karo. Deliver page mein export resolution 1080×1920 rakho aur "Use vertical resolution" toggle on karo. Baaki settings Reels/TikTok cheat sheet se follow karo.

MP4 ya QuickTime — social media exports ke liye kaunsa format better hai?

Social media ke liye MP4 safer default hai kyunki universally supported hai. QuickTime Mac pe equally accha kaam karta hai aur richer metadata preserve karta hai, lekin kuch Windows-based upload tools use kam reliably handle karte hain. YouTube ke liye dono formats identical result dete hain.
