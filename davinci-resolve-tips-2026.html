Node-Based Color Grading Explained

Unlike layer-based tools, DaVinci Resolve uses a node-based workflow where each node processes the image sequentially.

  • Serial Node: Applies corrections one after another.
  • Parallel Node: Combines independent looks.
  • Layer Node: Works like Photoshop layers.
  • Qualifier Node: Isolates color ranges.
