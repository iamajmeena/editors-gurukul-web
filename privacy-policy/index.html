Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This website respects your privacy. We do not sell your personal data, and we only collect information that is necessary for site operation, analytics, and improving the content we publish.

Information We Collect

When you visit the site, basic technical data such as browser type, approximate location, device type, and pages visited is collected automatically for site performance and analytics purposes. If you contact us directly (email, Instagram, YouTube), we use the information you share only to respond to your message — we do not share your details with third parties for marketing without your consent.

Cookies, Analytics & Google Translate

This site uses Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use the site — pages viewed, time on page, and general traffic sources. Google Analytics uses cookies and may collect your IP address and device information. You can opt out using browser extensions like Google's Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

The optional "हिंदी में पढ़ें" (Hindi translation) feature is powered by the Google Translate widget. Clicking it sets a cookie and loads a script from Google's servers to translate the page — Google's own privacy policy governs that data.

Some pages (for example, camera and gear guides) contain affiliate links — including Amazon Associates links. If you click through and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Product recommendations reflect our own testing and opinions, not sponsorship, unless explicitly stated on that page.

External links to YouTube, Instagram, and other third-party sites are provided for convenience — once you leave this site, that platform's own privacy policy applies.

Advertising

This site does not currently run third-party display ads. If that changes in the future, this policy will be updated to disclose the ad network used and how it uses cookies before ads go live.
